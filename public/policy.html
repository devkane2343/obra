🔐 Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Last Updated: January 1, 2025

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform ("the Site").

1 Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Personal Data: Name, email, contact number, address
  • Account Information: Username, password (hashed), profile data
  • Usage Data: Device type, browser, pages visited, clicks
  • Optional Data: Work category preferences, specialization, uploaded documents (certificates, licenses, etc.)

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To match workers and clients based on preferences and specialization
  • To send account-related updates
  • To improve platform features and security
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data to third parties.

3 Data Storage and Security

We store your information securely and only authorized personnel have access. Passwords are encrypted. Data is stored on secure servers (e.g., Firebase, Vercel, or equivalent hosting).

4 Sharing of Information

We may share your data:

  • With clients or workers only as required for project matching
  • With third-party services (e.g., cloud storage, analytics) bound by confidentiality agreements
  • If required by law

5 Your Rights

You may:

  • Access and update your profile
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

6 Cookies

We may use cookies to track site usage and improve user experience. You can disable them in your browser settings.

7 Contact Us

If you have questions, email us at privacy@obra.com.
